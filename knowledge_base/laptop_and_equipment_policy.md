# Acme Corp — Laptop and Equipment Policy

Document owner: IT Operations.

## Standard Issue

Every employee receives one company laptop on their first day. Engineering and Data roles receive a high-performance laptop; all other roles receive a standard laptop. Each employee may also request one external monitor, one keyboard, one mouse and one headset by raising an IT ticket in the "hardware" category.

## Laptop Refresh Cycle

Laptops are replaced on a fixed refresh cycle. Standard laptops are refreshed every 4 years. High-performance laptops used by Engineering and Data roles are refreshed every 3 years. Employees are notified by IT about 30 days before their refresh date. Early refresh is only granted when a device fails and cannot be economically repaired.

## Damaged Equipment

If your laptop is damaged, raise an IT ticket in the "hardware" category immediately. The first accidental damage incident in any 3-year period is repaired at no cost to the employee. For a second or subsequent incident in the same period, the employee contributes 25% of the repair cost, capped at $300.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours by raising an IT ticket in the "security" category with "critical" priority. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. A police report is required for stolen devices.

## Software

Only software from the Acme Software Catalog may be installed. Any software not in the catalog requires an IT ticket in the "software" category and approval from IT Security before installation. Personal licences may not be used on company devices.

## Returning Equipment

All equipment must be returned on or before the employee's last working day. IT will send a prepaid return box to remote employees.
