# Acme Corp — Business Travel Policy

Document owner: Finance and Workplace Services.

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the TravelDesk portal. Flights and hotels must be booked at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made outside TravelDesk are not reimbursed unless TravelDesk was unavailable.

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is permitted only for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 6 hours, and only with prior approval from a Vice President. Premium economy may be booked for flights over 4 hours with Director approval.

## Hotels

The hotel nightly rate cap is $200 per night. In high-cost cities — New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich and Singapore — the cap is $300 per night. Rates above the cap require Director approval before booking.

## Ground Transport

Taxis and ride-sharing are reimbursed for business purposes. Rental cars require manager pre-approval and must be the economy class or smaller. Mileage for personal cars is reimbursed at $0.60 per mile.

## Travel Insurance

All employees are covered by the Acme corporate travel insurance policy while on approved business travel. No separate insurance should be purchased.

## Meals While Travelling

Meal limits for business travel are defined in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.
