# Acme Corp — Remote Work Policy

Document owner: People Operations.

## Hybrid Working

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval. Teams agree on at least one common in-office "anchor day" each week.

## Fully Remote Roles

Fully remote working requires approval from both the employee's manager and their HR Business Partner, and is reviewed every 12 months.

## Working From Abroad

Employees may work from another country for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year, with prior approval from HR. This is required for tax and immigration compliance.

## Internet Stipend

Employees who work remotely at least 2 days per week receive an internet stipend of $50 per month, paid through payroll.

## Home Office Equipment

Acme does not pay a cash allowance for home office setup. Instead, remote and hybrid employees can request standard equipment — one external monitor, one keyboard, one mouse and one headset — by raising an IT ticket in the "hardware" category. Furniture such as desks and chairs is not provided or reimbursed.

## Availability

Remote employees must be reachable on Slack and email during their team's core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 local time.
