# Acme Corp — Leave Policy

Document owner: People Operations. Applies to all full-time employees.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service. New joiners receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their joining date. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

## Requesting Leave

Leave is requested through the HR Portal or through the Helpdesk Copilot. Requests for more than 3 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Requests of 3 days or fewer need 2 working days of notice. Your direct manager approves or rejects the request. Leave is not confirmed until it shows as "Approved" in the HR Portal.

## Carry-Forward of Unused Annual Leave

At the end of each calendar year, employees often ask what happens to the annual leave they did not use. The company recognises that business priorities sometimes make it difficult to take all of your leave, particularly during year-end releases, audits, and customer peak seasons. Managers are encouraged to plan coverage early in the year so that every team member can take a meaningful break, and People Operations publishes a reminder in October of every year listing each employee's remaining balance. Employees should review the reminder, speak to their manager about any planned time off in November and December, and update their requests in the HR Portal so that coverage can be arranged. Teams that have shared on-call responsibilities should agree the holiday rota before the end of October so that nobody loses time off because of scheduling conflicts.

Having considered the above, the rule is as follows. A maximum of 5 days may be moved into the following calendar year. These moved days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Any days above the 5-day limit lapse on 31 December and are not paid out, except where local law requires payment.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward. A medical certificate is required for any absence of more than 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave should be logged in the HR Portal on the day of absence or as soon as reasonably possible.

## Parental Leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be taken any time within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. Parental leave must be notified to People Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for an extended family member.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be approved by your manager and an HR Business Partner. Unpaid leave longer than 30 days requires Director approval.
