# Acme Corp — Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document owner: Finance. Applies to all employees and contractors with an Acme corporate card or reimbursement account.

## Submission Deadline

All expense claims must be submitted in the Expenses app within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected automatically and can only be reopened with written approval from the Finance Controller.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense above $25. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts. If a receipt is lost, the employee must complete a Missing Receipt Declaration, which can be used at most twice per calendar year.

## Approval Limits

Your direct manager approves claims up to $1,000 per claim. Claims between $1,000 and $5,000 additionally require Director approval. Claims above $5,000 require approval from the Finance Controller.

## Meals

Meal expenses while travelling on business are capped at $60 per day for domestic travel and $90 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at pre-approved client entertainment events. Team meals require pre-approval from a Director.

## Reimbursement Timing

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run, provided they are approved before the 20th of the month.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursed: traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment subscriptions, upgrades to premium seats without approval, minibar charges, and gifts to government officials.

## Home Office Expenses

Acme does not provide a cash home-office allowance and does not reimburse furniture purchased by employees. Remote employees receive equipment through the IT equipment process described in the Remote Work Policy and the Laptop and Equipment Policy.
