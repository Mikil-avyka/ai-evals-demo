# Acme Corp — Information Security Policy

Document owner: IT Security.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords for Acme accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all Acme systems. Passwords must never be shared with anyone — including IT staff, managers or the Helpdesk Copilot. IT will never ask you for your password.

## Phishing

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links or open attachments. Use the "Report Phish" button in Outlook, or forward the email to the IT Security team. If you have already clicked a link or entered credentials, raise an IT ticket in the "security" category with "critical" priority immediately.

## Security Incidents

Any suspected security incident must be reported within 1 hour of discovery by raising an IT ticket in the "security" category. Examples include malware alerts, unexpected MFA prompts, and data sent to the wrong recipient.

## Data Handling

Customer data and employee personal data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. Employee personal data (for example home address, personal phone number, salary, or leave records) may only be accessed by the employee themselves, their direct manager, or authorised HR staff, and only for a legitimate business purpose.

## Access Requests

Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis. Access requests are raised as IT tickets in the "access" category and must be approved by the system owner.

## Acceptable Use

Company devices are for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable provided it does not breach this policy or interfere with work.
